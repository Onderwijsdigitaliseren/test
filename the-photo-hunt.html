The Photo Hunt

no map. your camera is the evidence.

The killer walked through this neighbourhood last night and left things behind. Not at a place, but at a kind of place: a bench, a letterbox, a manhole cover. Find them. Photograph them. Read what was lying there.

  • Eight photo tasks, doable anywhere. The phone is watching.
  • Five suspects, four witnesses, one liar.
  • At the end: your evidence strip, to share.
–0 Day streak0 Solved

  1. You get no location but a task: “Photograph a letterbox.” Which one is up to you. It works in any street, and in any house. Your phone recognises what is in the photo by itself; a photo of something else does not count.
  2. Once the photo is taken, you see what the killer left there, or what a witness saw. Evidence is always true; one of the witnesses lies.
  3. After eight photos you name the killer and the liar. Your photos become one evidence strip.

Your photos stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Outdoors: stay on public roads, don't photograph people.
