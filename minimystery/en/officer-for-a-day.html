Officer for a Day

All units. Every street sign has been stolen.

Today you are the police. Follow eight leads, solve eight police puzzles and work out which of the five suspects has the street signs. Then: the line-up, the handcuffs and a real police diploma. For ages 6 to 10; pick the age and the puzzles adjust.

–0 Day streak0 Caught

  1. With map: the chief pins eight leads on places nearby. Walk there; a police puzzle is waiting. Without map or indoors: you get the puzzles one after another, without places. Works anywhere.
  2. Pick the age: for 8 and up the puzzles are longer and one witness lies. The puzzles: taking a fingerprint, making a composite sketch, recognising a footprint, copying the siren, spotting differences through the binoculars, a colour code on the radio, a chase and finding the crook in a crowd. No reading needed: the phone reads everything aloud (Read aloud button).
  3. After every puzzle you get a street sign back and a lead. Evidence is always true and shows a feature: hat, shirt or shoes. Witnesses help too, but they sometimes get it wrong: only the evidence really counts.
  4. After eight leads you go to the line-up: five suspects in a row in 3D. Tap the culprit. Handcuffs on, a confession, and the group gets a police diploma and a WANTED poster with their own photos, as PDFs to print.

Tip for the grown-up: read the evidence aloud to the whole group, that is the best moment. For six-year-olds Indoors or Outside without map works best; the map mode is for children who can read a little. With an adult along. One phone per group; with the case code more phones play the same case. The photos stay on the phone. Outside: stay on the pavement.
