How it works

The same MiniMystery formula as the party games, with a real school subject, small investigations in your own neighbourhood and a lesson plan for the teacher.

1

The leader starts the case

Choose School or After-school, the age and the length (all spots close together). You get a case code for all teams and a leader's sheet with the story, all answers and hints.

2

Teams play

Teams of 4–6 children, one phone per team: enter the case code and walk. At every spot a short educational task: counting, reading, looking, thinking. One hint per task. With map, without map or inside the building.

3

Finale and certificate

The keys the teams collected solve the mystery in the finale. Then the reveal and a certificate (A4) with the team name. The leader keeps track of the teams on the leader's sheet.

The adventures

One adventure per subject: nature, English literacy, maths, history, and a light version for after-school clubs. Playable around any school, and also inside the building with spot cards. No app, no physical kit.

Nature · ages 6–12 · new

The Missing Bees

The beehive behind the school is empty. Bees only stay where there are enough flowers, water and places to shelter. At nature stations around (or inside) the school, teams make real observations to find out what is missing.

  • Counting flowers, recognising trees, minibeasts under a stone, one minute of listening to birds, water, soil, weather, litter, animal tracks, the season, shelters, pollination, stone or green, who eats whom
  • Three levels (ages 6–8, 8–10, 10–12); every team gets its own count to compare in class, and a fun fact after every answer
  • Finale: the cycle in order (sun → plant → animal → soil); lesson plan per age group, certificate
  • 6, 8 or 10 stations
  • 35–60 min
  • Outdoors (or at the window)
  • Case code
  • Lesson plan

English literacy · ages 6–12 · new

The Letter Thief

The sign on the wall says S_HO_L: the Letter Thief has stolen letters. At word stations around (or inside) the school, teams read, spell and build with real words from the neighbourhood.

  • Copying the street sign and counting letters, vowels or syllables, rhyming with what you see, compound words, opposites, word classes, spelling, the alphabet, punctuation, building a sentence, plurals, figurative language, loanwords, a note to the thief
  • Three levels (ages 6–8, 8–10, 10–12), a language tip after every answer
  • Finale: the word keys in order to make the Letter Thief's sentence; lesson plan per age group, certificate
  • 6, 8 or 10 stations
  • 35–60 min

Maths · ages 6–12 · new

Professor Zero and the Missing Numbers

All the numbers in the school are gone. Professor Zero thinks numbers are pointless. At maths stations around (or inside) the school, teams prove him wrong with real measurements.

  • Counting steps, windows, house numbers, lamp posts, the clock without numbers, the till, counting traffic, paving stones, sharing fairly, estimating height, shapes, scale
  • The team types what it counted itself; the follow-up question calculates with it. A maths tip after every answer
  • Three levels (ages 6–8, 8–10, 10–12), lesson plan per age group, certificate
  • 6, 8 or 10 stations
  • 35–60 min

History · ages 8–12 · new

The Time Travellers

The clock in the hall is a time machine, and it has been wound the wrong way. There are now time leaks around (or inside) the school. At each leak the team looks into an era, does an investigation in its own neighbourhood and earns a time key. Finale: the eras in order.

  • The ten eras of Dutch history, from hunters and farmers to television and computers (Long: also now and the future)
  • Per era: a short time journey, an investigation (looking for traces, counting, comparing), a question at the right level, a "did you know" and the neighbourhood check
  • Lesson plan for before and after, Dutch core objectives 51–53, certificate with the team's timeline
  • 6, 10 or 12 eras
  • 35–60 min
  • Outdoors or in the building

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

The School Pass costs € 39.99 and is valid for six months. All your classes and teams play every educational walk as often as you like. You buy it via Ko-fi and get a code straight away. Enter it on every device a team plays on. The practice round is free.

What do I need?

One phone with internet per team (and location switched on for the map). Nothing else: no app, no account. Just the leader's case code. You can print the leader's sheet, but you don't have to.

How much preparation does it take?

A minute: start the case, share the case code. The leader's sheet has the story to read aloud and all the answers and hints for exactly this case.

Do the children have to walk far?

No. Outdoors, all spots are within about 300 metres of the start. And every adventure can also be played entirely inside the building: you choose spots in the school, print a card with a code for each spot, and the teams walk from spot to spot.

Where do the photos go?

They stay on the team's device. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I get my own story or theme?

Yes, custom versions are possible. Get in touch.
