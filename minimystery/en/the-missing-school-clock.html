The Mystery of the Missing School Clock

The big clock isn't there any more!

This morning the school clock was gone. Only a note was left behind: six letters show the way, the numbers open the door. At eight, ten or twelve spots around your school or after-school club there is a case: counting, reading, looking, thinking. Teams of 4–6 children, one phone per team.

–0 Days in a row0 Solved
Who is playing

  1. For the leader: choose School or After-school, the age and the length (8, 10 or 12 spots, all within about 300 metres of the start). Start the case on your own phone: the briefing shows the case code. Every team enters that code (button Enter a case code) and gets exactly the same case. Below the briefing is the leader's sheet with all answers and hints, for printing.
  2. With map: Mr Tik pins cases on spots in the neighbourhood. The children see a story map: their group walks towards the next spot, as far as they really got closer, with an arrow and cold/warm/hot. The leader can tap the button to look at the real map. Without map: the cases come one after another; after each case the team walks to a new spot by itself. Indoors: walking on the spot with the phone in your hand.
  3. Each case is a short educational task that fits the spot: knowledge about the place (nature, time, history, money), looking and counting (counting windows, house numbers, measuring steps), language and reading (street sign, secret code, spelling) and traffic and safety. Three levels: 6–7, 8–9 and 10–12 years. In After-school mode each case starts with a movement task; in School mode the learning goals are shown.
  4. Right answer = a letter or a number. One hint per case. One photo task (the living clock); the photo stays on the phone.
  5. After all the cases: the six letters spell the place, the numbers open the padlock. Then the reveal and a certificate (A4) with the team name.

Tip: agree on the play area and a finish time. Teams stay together and on the pavement. No account, no app: it works in the browser.
