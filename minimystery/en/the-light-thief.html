The Light Thief

Last night at 03:03 someone stole every light in the street. And the present.

The Light Thief is hiding at five, eight or eleven places nearby: you choose. Catch him and he drops a light out of fright. Every puzzle also gives a golden digit. All the lights in your lantern and you see who he is by day. With the golden digits you crack the lock of his hideout. That is where the present is.

How long?
Puzzles

  1. With map: the Light Thief hides at five, eight or eleven places nearby and creeps across the map. Walk there and catch him. No map or indoors: you get the challenges one after another, without places. Works anywhere.
  2. Every time he dares you differently: a photo (a bench, a letterbox, the whole group as shadows; the phone checks whether it fits) or a game on the screen: catch him, find him with the torch, a puzzle, a lock, guessing shadows, counting lights, lighting lanterns, memory, finding his eyes in the dark. Every puzzle gives a golden digit. Choose Easy (pictures, the phone reads everything aloud) or Tricky.
  3. If you succeed he flees, drops a light and shouts something. Sometimes he slips up: that is evidence and always true. Sometimes he claims something about himself: all true except one lie.
  4. With all the lights your lantern is bright enough. Point out who he is and which claim was the lie. Open the lock of his hideout with the golden digits and find the present. Your photos become an album and a diploma.

With an adult along. One phone per group; with the hunt code several phones play the same hunt. The photos stay on the phone. Outside: stay on the pavement, do not photograph strangers.
