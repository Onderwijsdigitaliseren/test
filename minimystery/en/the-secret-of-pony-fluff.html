The Secret of Pony Fluff

The stable is empty. The gate was open!

Pony Fluff is gone. Follow the hoof prints, solve stable puzzles, collect golden horseshoes and ask the animals: the goat, the duck, the dog, the cat and the crow. One of them has it wrong. Where is Fluff?

  1. Short, Normal or Long: 5, 8 or 11 places (about half an hour, 45 minutes or an hour and a quarter). The length is part of the case code, so other phones get the same case.
  2. With map: Fenna pins hoof prints on places nearby. Walk there; a puzzle is waiting. The children see a storybook map of the land around the stables: your group walks along the road to the next place, as far as you've really got closer, with an arrow and cold/warm/hot. The grown-up can peek at the real map with the button. Now and then Fluff trots across the screen: tap quickly, three glimpses earn a sticker on the passport. GPS not working? The same case simply continues without the map. The children don't see a street map but a detective compass: an arrow and cold/warm/hot. The grown-up can peek at the map with the button. Now and then Fluff trots past: tap quickly, three glimpses earn a sticker on the passport. GPS not working? Then the same case simply carries on without the map. Without map or indoors: you get the puzzles one after another, without places. Works anywhere.
  3. Foal is easy: short puzzles and one animal gets it wrong. Rider is trickier: longer puzzles and one animal really lies. No reading needed: the phone reads everything aloud (Read aloud button).
  4. The puzzles: finding the hoof print in the sand, looking through the binoculars, grooming a photo, counting apples, remembering the halter colours, the crow's shinies, petting Kato the cat, taking Fluff through the meadow, guessing animal sounds, show jumping, animal memory and braiding the mane.
  5. After every puzzle you get a golden horseshoe with a number, and a lead. Traces are always true and point at a feature: the flag, the animals or the treats of the place where Bibi is staying. The animals help too, but not all of them well.
  6. After the last lead, Karel the crow has locked the gate. The golden numbers open it. Then you choose from five places where Fluff is. Kato the cat tells the secret of the open gate, and the group gets an album with all the photos and a Rider's Passport as a PDF to print.

Tip for the grown-up: read the traces aloud to the whole group, that is the best moment. One phone per group; with the case code more phones play the same case. The photos stay on the phone. Outside: stay on the pavement.
