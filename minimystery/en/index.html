How to play

How it works

No app, no account. Just open it in the browser.

  1. OutdoorsA treasure hunt places spots in your own neighbourhood. Walk there; a task is waiting.
  2. Puzzles & gamesRiddles, group photos, spot-the-difference, shaking and blowing. Everyone joins in.
  3. Or indoorsIn the living room or the garden: without a map, or with the phone as an enchanted mirror.
  4. DiplomaCase solved? You get a diploma with your photos, to keep or to print.
Tips for the grown-up
  • One phone per group is enough. Playing with more phones? Enter the same Party Pass code on each phone, then the case code. It is shown on the first phone at the start of the case and in the notebook.
  • Read the clues and riddles aloud to the whole group: that is the best moment.
  • Playing outside? Stay on the pavement; an adult always walks along.
  • Allow 40 to 75 minutes.
