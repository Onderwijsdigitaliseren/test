Pick your case

Three mysteries, each with its own story. With the Party Pass you play them for real: in your own streets, without a map, or indoors.

How to play

How it works

No app, no account. Just open it in the browser.

  1. With a mapThe case places eight spots in your own neighbourhood. Walk there; a task is waiting.
  2. Photos & puzzlesGroup photos, spot-the-difference and games on screen. Everyone joins in.
  3. Or indoorsRather stay in? Play without a map or simply at home.
  4. DiplomaCase solved? You get a diploma with your photos, as a PDF to print.
Tips for the grown-up
  • One phone per group is enough. With the case code more phones play the same case.
  • Read the clues aloud to the whole group: that is the best moment.
  • Outside, stay on the pavement. An adult always walks along.
  • Allow 40 to 75 minutes, cake break included.
