Enigma Mystery

Enigma Mystery brings together every murder mystery that appeared earlier as The Graveyard Files, Het Nachtdossier, Murder Walk and Moordwandeling. Every case is fiction, every puzzle is checked: there is exactly one solution and the clues give you exactly enough.

Privacy

How your data is handled

The games. Everything runs in your browser. Your progress, hints used, your language and your Pro code are kept in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device; clearing your browser data removes them. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

The note form. Only if you send a note do we receive what you typed: your message, and your name and e-mail address if you gave them. It arrives as an e-mail through FormSubmit, which does not keep your message afterwards. We use it only to read and reply, and share it with nobody.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, logs IP addresses for security. We do not see or use those logs.

The Pro pass. Purchases go through Ko-fi, under Ko-fi's own privacy terms. Nothing about a purchase comes back to this site.

Who is responsible. Kellie van de Kerkhof, reachable at kellievandekerkhof@gmail.com. Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
