How it works

Three steps, one killer

  1. I

    Pick a case

    Start where you stand. The map places eight leads at real places within walking distance: the church, the bus stop, the café.

  2. II

    Walk and collect

    Within 35 metres a lead opens: a shade who saw something, a piece of evidence, a camera still, a message.

  3. III

    Name the killer

    Rule out suspects together in the notebook, unmask the liar and name the killer.
