Questions
What does personalising cost?

€ 29.95 including the ticket per walk or game. For that you get your own version with photos, names and a theme.

Can the route be different?

No, when personalising the case stays as it is. A route, story or new parts of your own count as something new: send a request and you'll hear what it costs.

Can I still change things afterwards?

You can ask for one round of changes once you've seen your version. Then it's final.

How far ahead should I ask?

At least two weeks ahead. For something entirely new, longer.
