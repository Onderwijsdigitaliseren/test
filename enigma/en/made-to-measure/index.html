Teams racing each other through town, the boss as prime suspect (tongue in cheek) and a winner at the end.

Hen or stag party

The bride or groom in the lead role. A route past your places, finishing at the drinks.

Family or friends' day

Grandad against the grandchildren, the old friends against each other. Playable for all ages.

Event or tourism

How it works

Three steps to your own case

  1. I

    Pick a walk and tell me what you want

    Fill in the form: which walk, the occasion, number of players, place and your ideas. Put everything you want in straight away.

  2. II

    You get a proposal with a fixed price

    You hear what's possible and what it costs. Nothing is fixed until you agree.

  3. III

    I rebuild it, you check it once

    After the rebuild you see the case and can send one round of changes. Then it's final and you get your own link and codes for every team.

Questions
What does a custom case cost?

You get a proposal with a fixed price up front. It depends on the group and what needs rebuilding.

Can I still change things afterwards?

After the rebuild there is one round of changes. Then the case is final. Want something different later? That's new work at a new price.

How far ahead should I ask?

Ideally two to four weeks. In a hurry? Just ask, often more is possible than you think.

Does someone come along?

No, you play yourselves on your own phones. That keeps it affordable and you start whenever you like.

Can it be played without walking outside?

Yes. A case can also be played indoors: you walk on your steps instead of GPS.
