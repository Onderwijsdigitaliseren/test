For which occasion?

What a custom case is for

Company or team outing

Teams racing each other through town, the boss as prime suspect (tongue in cheek) and a winner at the end.

Hen or stag party

The bride or groom in the lead role. A route past your places, finishing at the drinks.

Birthday or anniversary

The birthday person as victim, suspect or detective. With references only you will get.

Family or friends' day

Grandad against the grandchildren, the old friends against each other. Playable for all ages.

School or class

A lesson or induction day as a murder case: teamwork, logical reasoning, getting to know the town.

Event or tourism

A permanent case for your town, hotel, festival or club that visitors can walk themselves.

What can be tailored

It's all about you

Your story

Victim, suspects and motive fit your group: the office, the wedding, the family, the class.

Your names

Colleagues, friends or relatives as witnesses and suspects. Who did it?

Your route

Start at the station, office or hotel and finish at the café or restaurant you were going to anyway.

Your group

From two people to a hundred: in teams against each other, with time, points and a leaderboard.

Your language

In English or Dutch, mixed for international teams too.

Your moment

On the day itself, or a case your guests can walk on their own for weeks.

Questions
What does a custom case cost?

It depends on the size of the group and how much needs tailoring. You always get a proposal with a fixed price first.

How far ahead should I ask?

Ideally two to four weeks. In a hurry? Just ask, often more is possible than you think.

Does someone come along?

No, you play yourselves on your own phones. That keeps it affordable and you start whenever you like.

Can it be played without walking outside?

Yes. A case can also be played indoors: you walk on your steps instead of GPS.
