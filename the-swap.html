The Swap

Last night at 03:03 all the lights went out. Then the present was gone.

The Shadow Thief has hidden the present in his hideout. Trade with him eight times, put his mirror together and find out who he is by day. Then you know where the present is.

–0 Days in a row0 Caught

  1. With map: the Shadow Thief picks eight places nearby and creeps across the map. Walk there; the swap is waiting. No map or indoors: you get the swaps one after another, without places. Works anywhere.
  2. Sometimes the swap is a photo (a bench, a letterbox, the whole group as shadows); the phone checks whether it fits. Sometimes it is a game on the screen: catch him, find him with the torch, a puzzle, a lock.
  3. After every swap you get a mirror shard and a message. Sometimes he slips up: that is evidence and always true. Sometimes he claims something about himself: three times true, once a lie.
  4. After eight shards you point out who he is and which claim was the lie. Then you enter his hideout to find the present. Your photos become an evidence strip and a diploma.

With an adult along. One phone per group; with the swap code several phones play the same swap. The photos stay on the phone. Outside: stay on the pavement, do not photograph strangers.
