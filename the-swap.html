The Swap

The Magpie has the present. You get it back. Maybe.

The Magpie has pinched the birthday present and is sending messages. He wants to trade: you do a task, he gives something back. Eight times. But he lies. Once.

  • Eight swaps: photos of places nearby and silly group photos.
  • The Magpie slips up three times. Four times he claims something. Once he lies.
  • At the end: which of the five neighbours is The Magpie? Catch him.
–0 Days in a row0 Caught

  1. With map: The Magpie picks eight places nearby. Walk there; that is where you get his task. No map or indoors: you only get tasks, no places. Works anywhere.
  2. Every task is a photo: of a kind of place (a bench, a letterbox) or of the whole group (as angry magpies, on one leg). The phone checks whether it fits.
  3. After every photo The Magpie replies. Sometimes he slips up: that is evidence and always true. Sometimes he claims something about himself: three times true, once a lie.
  4. After eight swaps you point out who The Magpie is and which claim was the lie. Your photos become one evidence strip.

For ages 8 to 11, with an adult along. One phone per group; with the swap code several phones play the same swap. The photos stay on the phone. Outside: stay on the pavement, do not photograph strangers.
